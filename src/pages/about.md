I write documentation that helps people use and evaluate complex systems: model cards, tutorials, API
references, and evaluation guides. My background is in regulated healthcare documentation. As an intern
at Gainwell Technologies, I wrote and edited HIPAA EDI companion guides for DC Medicaid trading partners.
The guides are public: [DC DHCF companion guides](https://github.com/kopyk/gainwell-dhcf-companion-guides).

I have a BA in English from the University of Houston.

## About these samples

All samples are original work written for this portfolio. Systems, part numbers and data are fictional.
No CUI, export-controlled (ITAR/EAR) or proprietary information is included.

The [ask-my-docs](/projects) tool is the exception: it's real, and so are its evaluation results.
